Services

Semantic Search & Embedding Systems

Build search that understands what people mean, not just the words they type. I help design and improve embedding-based retrieval and ranking systems, from model selection and data preparation to evaluation against real search needs.

Customer value: Help users find relevant results more easily, improve search quality, and give your team clear ways to measure progress.

Fine-Tuning for Your Data

Adapt pretrained models to your domain, language, and tasks. I can help assess your data and goals, select a suitable base model, build the fine-tuning pipeline, and evaluate the results.

Customer value: Get a model better suited to your use case, with improvements measured against your business goals.

Low-Rank Generative Modeling

Explore efficient generative models with low-rank tensor methods. My research focuses on Tensor Trains, DMRG, and rectified flow matching—approaches for representing complex data and generative dynamics with compact models.

Customer value: Investigate ways to reduce model size and computational demands, making advanced generative modeling more practical to test and apply.
